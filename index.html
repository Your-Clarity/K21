Map: Dunstable Downs — the Chiltern escarpment runs NE–SW and faces NW. With a NW wind you get ridge lift along the slope; thermals drift downwind. London GC sits at the foot.

Controls (iPad): the iPad IS the stick — tilt to roll & pitch (tap SET NEUTRAL to zero). Pedals bottom-centre are your feet: push one, it holds. Left wall: blue airbrake and green trim, like the real front cockpit.
Controls (Mac): hold the mouse button and move = stick (right hand); release and it springs back to trim. Keyboard: A/D rudder (holds), S centre rudder, ↑/↓ airbrake, T/G trim, E engine.

View: you fly from the front seat (P1, the student's seat) — canopy frame around you, glare shield below. CHASE VIEW shows the whole aircraft with moving surfaces.

Honest seams: mass/area/polar/stall are from real data; the stability derivatives & inertias are textbook estimates tuned to the POH's docile handling. No g-feel or buffet. Mental rehearsal only — your London GC instructors are the authority.
